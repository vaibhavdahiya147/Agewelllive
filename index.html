A note to families caring from afar

“Who’s keeping track of Mum and Dad’s healthcare when I’m not there?”

If you live away from your parents, you may know that worry well.

You call regularly. You save prescription photos. You remind them about appointments.

Then a medicine changes. A report arrives. One doctor asks for a follow-up in six weeks, another in three months.

You scroll through family chats, trying to find the latest instructions.

Your parents are managing their day. You’re managing yours. Everyone is making an effort, but keeping the details connected takes time.

Sometimes, you finish a call and remember the question you meant to ask.

Was the refill ordered? Did that appointment happen? Which prescription are they following now?

You want to stay involved while respecting the routines and independence they value.

And you want your calls home to have room for the everyday things, too—the neighbour’s visit, the evening walk, what’s for dinner.

That’s why we’re building AgeWell.

One up-to-date record of medicines and follow-ups, simple reminders through familiar channels, and a named care coordinator who follows up when something slips.

Your parents choose what they share. You see what’s done and what needs attention.

We’re opening our early access waitlist and would love to build AgeWell with families who know this experience.
